Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 26, 2026  |  Package ID: com.solidtech.storepilot

This Privacy Policy explains how Store Pilot (developed and maintained by SOLID Tech) collects, uses, stores, and protects your information when you use the Store Pilot mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide store management, billing, and cloud backup features, Store Pilot collects only the information you provide or authorize:

  • Account & Sign-In Information: Email address, authentication credentials managed securely by Firebase Authentication, and—if you choose Google Sign-In—your Google account email, display name, and profile photo URL.
  • Store & Business Profile: Shop name, owner name, business phone number(s), and shop address entered during registration or edited in My Profile.
  • Business & Operational Records: Products, stock batches, buying/selling prices, cost-code mappings, sales invoices, customer due records, return records, and product photos you add to manage your shop.

2. Device Permissions

Store Pilot requests device permissions only when needed for specific user-initiated actions:

  • Camera: Used solely to scan product barcodes, scan receipt QR codes for verification, or capture product photos.
  • Storage / Media Access: Used solely to pick product images from your device gallery and to save, print, or share PDF receipts, reports, and database backups.
  • Internet Access: Required for account authentication and syncing your store data with Google Cloud Firestore.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account and protect access to your store workspace.
  • Store and sync your inventory, POS sales, due balances, returns, and reports across sessions.
  • Generate PDF invoices, receipts with verification QR codes, and financial summary reports.
  • Respond to support or account deletion inquiries sent to our contact email.

Store Pilot does not display third-party advertisements and does not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business data to third parties.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

Store Pilot relies on trusted Google Cloud / Firebase infrastructure to deliver authentication and real-time database storage:

  • Google Firebase Authentication & Google Sign-In: Used to authenticate users securely.
  • Google Cloud Firestore: Used to store your isolated shop records securely under your account ID.

These services process data according to Google's Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy.

5. Data Security & Retention

All communication between the Store Pilot app and Firebase services is encrypted in transit via HTTPS/TLS. Each store's data is isolated under its owner account ID, and we retain your data for as long as your account remains active.

6. Account & Data Deletion

You can permanently delete your Store Pilot account and all associated store data (profile, products, stock entries, sales records, invoices, returns, images, and settings) directly inside the app via: My Profile → Delete Account.

You may also request account deletion or privacy assistance anytime by contacting us at jakaria.cse.pust@gmail.com.

7. Children's Privacy

Store Pilot is a business inventory and point-of-sale application and is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
