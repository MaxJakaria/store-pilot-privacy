Account & Data Deletion Request

App: Store Pilot  |  Developer: SOLID Tech  |  Package ID: com.solidtech.storepilot

You have full control over your Store Pilot account and shop data. You can permanently delete your account either directly inside the app (instant) or by submitting a deletion request below.

Option 1: Delete Immediately Inside the App

  1. Open the Store Pilot app and sign in to your account.
  2. Tap the profile icon at the top-right corner to open the menu, then select My Profile.
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete Account.
  4. Confirm deletion when prompted. Your account and all store records are permanently erased immediately.

Option 2: Request Deletion via Email (Without App Access)

If you have uninstalled Store Pilot or cannot access your account, you can request full account and data deletion via email:

  1. Send an email from your registered Store Pilot email address to jakaria.cse.pust@gmail.com.
  2. Use the subject line: Delete Store Pilot Account.
  3. Include your registered email address and shop name so we can verify ownership.
  4. We will verify and permanently delete your account and associated data within 7 business days.
Request Account Deletion via Email

What Data Is Deleted

Deleting your Store Pilot account permanently erases:

  • Your authentication account (email/password and linked Google Sign-In credentials in Firebase Authentication).
  • Your user profile and store profile (shop name, owner name, phone numbers, and address).
  • All store operational records: products, stock batches, sales records, invoices, due balances, return records, product images, cost-code settings, and counters.

Data Retention

Once account deletion is completed, no personal or store data is retained in our active Firebase Authentication or Cloud Firestore databases.
